The PSP frontend adapts FrogGBA's green/cream palette, vertical menu,
two-column file browser, recent-game concept, clock/battery header and help
strip. FrogGBA UI enhancements are by tzubertowski (Prosty) and contributors.
Original gui.c notices: Copyright (C) 2006 Exophase; (C) 2007 takka.
FrogGBA project: Copyright (C) 2024 FrogGBA Project Contributors.
The upstream license notice is preserved in FrogGBA.txt.

The N64 core remains DaedalusX64, with its original author notices intact.
No GBA emulation core or FrogGBA bitmap fonts are included in FrogN64.
The frog mark is newly drawn from rectangles for this project.
PSP fonts are loaded from the device firmware, as in DaedalusX64.
